## Conclusion
Benchmarks are the parenting manual for AI products: each one isolates a different skill, from writing a ten-line function to reasoning through a novel puzzle to closing a real support ticket. No single score tells the whole story, so read them as a panel, not a leaderboard. Pick the benchmarks that mirror what your users will actually ask the model to do and keep on improving.
